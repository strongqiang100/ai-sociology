# AI Sociology

## Human Finitude, Expanding Generation, and the Reorganization of Social Order

**An open theoretical framework and research toolkit**

[中文](README.md) · [Foundations](theory/01-foundations.en.md) · [Five-volume index](theory/README.md) · [References](theory/REFERENCES.md)

How do time, choice, relationships and work change as answers, images and services become easier to obtain?

When reports become quicker to write, do they become easier to review? Who controls the time saved at work? As content becomes cheaper to produce, whose work still gets seen?

These questions lead into an examination of time, attention, emotion, work, relationships and distribution. Each argument retains its conditions and considers circumstances in which the result could reverse.

**Research edition 0.1.0 · 2026-09-22 · Working paper**

16 principles · 16 domains · 128 variables · 64 conditional paths · 8 formal models · Chinese and English

This is an open research framework. Derivations state assumptions; unvalidated paths remain hypotheses rather than predetermined conclusions.

## Core questions

**Finitude.** Time, attention and recovery are bounded, while capacities, support and control can change.

**Conditional devaluation.** Generation prices, formal rarity, signal informativeness and skill returns are distinct; task prices do not measure human worth.

**Branching mechanisms.** The same capability gain may produce opposite results under different demand, ownership, bargaining and institutions.

**Open conclusions.** Important derivations require boundaries, alternatives and observations that could weaken them.

## Continue the research

Run from the repository root using standard-library Python:

```bash
python3 scripts/explore_theory.py --stats
python3 scripts/explore_theory.py --variables V009 V057 V098 V097 --order 3 --limit 4
python3 scripts/check_theory.py
```

The enumerator covers **11,017,504** unordered candidate tuples of orders two through four. Candidates are not derived or validated causal relationships. Develop mechanisms from the [64 cards](theory/03-derivation-pool.en.md) and [proposal template](theory/templates/research-proposal.md).
