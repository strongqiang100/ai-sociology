# 参考文献与使用边界 / References and scope of use

本表列出原始理论、原始研究或作者发布的原文。链接核查日期：2026-09-22。编号用于定位，未按出现顺序排列。文献支持相邻概念与方法；本项目中的 AI 场景推演仍是本项目提出的条件性假说。书目、模型和证据强度不应混为一谈。

The list contains original theory, original research or author-issued texts. Links were checked on 2026-09-22. IDs are stable locators, not order-of-appearance numbers. Sources support adjacent concepts and methods; the AI-specific derivations remain this project's conditional hypotheses.

## R01 · 信息与注意 / Information and attention

Simon, H. A. (1971). *Designing Organizations for an Information-Rich World*. In M. Greenberger (Ed.), *Computers, Communications, and the Public Interest*. Johns Hopkins Press. [原文扫描 / Original text](https://gwern.net/doc/design/1971-simon.pdf).

**用途：** 信息丰富环境中的注意配置问题。**边界：** 不提供现代平台的固定时长定律，也不证明个体注意能力不能改善。

**Use:** Attention allocation under information abundance. **Limit:** Not a fixed-duration law for platforms or proof of unchangeable capacity.

## R02 · 自我损耗的重复研究 / Ego-depletion replication

Hagger, M. S., et al. (2016). A Multilab Preregistered Replication of the Ego-Depletion Effect. *Perspectives on Psychological Science, 11*(4), 546–573. [原始论文 / Article](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616652873).

**用途：** 提醒“固定可耗尽心理资源”需要实证审查。**边界：** 单一重复研究既不证明所有疲劳不存在，也不解决全部情绪调节机制。

**Use:** Scrutiny of fixed-resource explanations. **Limit:** Neither denial of fatigue nor a complete theory of emotion regulation.

## R03 · 信息不对称 / Asymmetric information

Akerlof, G. A. (1970). The Market for “Lemons”: Quality Uncertainty and the Market Mechanism. *The Quarterly Journal of Economics, 84*(3), 488–500. [原文 / Original text](https://www.sfu.ca/~allen/Ackerlof.pdf).

**用途：** 质量难识别时的交易机制。**边界：** 本项目将其概念用于研究内容和核验，不声称所有生成内容市场必然逆向选择或崩溃。

**Use:** Exchange under quality uncertainty. **Limit:** No claim that all generated-content markets necessarily unravel.

## R04 · 信号 / Signaling

Spence, M. (1973). Job Market Signaling. *The Quarterly Journal of Economics, 87*(3), 355–374. [原始论文 / Article](https://doi.org/10.2307/1882010).

**用途：** 信号成本、类型区分和接收者判断的关系。**边界：** 形式精美不必然是该模型意义上的分离信号；是否区分质量需测量。

**Use:** Signal costs and type differentiation. **Limit:** Polish is not automatically a separating signal.

## R05 · 社会比较 / Social comparison

Festinger, L. (1954). A Theory of Social Comparison Processes. *Human Relations, 7*(2), 117–140. [原始论文 / Article](https://doi.org/10.1177/001872675400700202).

**用途：** 比较对象如何影响评价。**边界：** 不推出人只能追求相对名次，也不证明社会性需求永不饱和。

**Use:** Comparison processes in evaluation. **Limit:** Not a theory that all motives reduce to rank or never saturate.

## R06 · 自主、胜任与关系 / Autonomy, competence and relatedness

Ryan, R. M., & Deci, E. L. (2000). Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being. *American Psychologist, 55*(1), 68–78. [作者发布原文 / Author-hosted text](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf).

**用途：** 区分自主性、胜任感与关系需要。**边界：** 不是本项目全部 AI 使用、陪伴或焦虑路径的直接实验依据。

**Use:** Distinguishing autonomy, competence and relatedness. **Limit:** Not direct experimental validation of this project's AI-use or companionship hypotheses.

## R07 · 自动化与监督 / Automation and oversight

Bainbridge, L. (1983). Ironies of Automation. *Automatica, 19*(6), 775–779. [原始论文 / Article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0005109883900468) · [原文扫描 / Original text](https://gwern.net/doc/sociology/technology/1983-bainbridge.pdf).

**用途：** 自动化后留给人的监控与接管任务。**边界：** 不宣称所有人类审核必然无效，也不把论文当作生成式 AI 的当代评测。

**Use:** Monitoring and intervention after automation. **Limit:** Neither universal review failure nor a contemporary generative-AI benchmark.

## R08 · 享乐适应 / Hedonic adaptation

Frederick, S., & Loewenstein, G. (1999). Hedonic Adaptation. In *Well-Being: The Foundations of Hedonic Psychology*. [作者发布章节 / Author-hosted chapter](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/HedonicAdaptation.pdf).

**用途：** 体验对重复和生活变化的适应。**边界：** 不认为适应完整、均匀或不可改变；刺激竞赛是进一步假说。

**Use:** Adaptation to repetition and changed circumstances. **Limit:** Adaptation need not be complete, uniform or immutable; stimulation races are further hypotheses.

## R09 · 自动化、新任务与劳动 / Automation, new tasks and labor

Acemoglu, D., & Restrepo, P. (2019). Automation and New Tasks: How Technology Displaces and Reinstates Labor. *Journal of Economic Perspectives, 33*(2), 3–30. [作者所在机构原文 / Institutional text](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/Automation%20and%20New%20Tasks%20-%20How%20Technology%20Displaces%20and%20Reinstates%20Labor.pdf).

**用途：** 任务替代、劳动需求和新任务的区分。**边界：** 不提供本项目各职业的具体替代率；M05 是另行明确假设的简化模型。

**Use:** Distinguishing displacement, labor demand and new tasks. **Limit:** No occupation-specific replacement rates; M05 is a separately specified simplification.

## R10 · 社会影响与文化市场 / Social influence in cultural markets

Salganik, M. J., Dodds, P. S., & Watts, D. J. (2006). Experimental Study of Inequality and Unpredictability in an Artificial Cultural Market. *Science, 311*(5762), 854–856. [作者发布原文及补充材料 / Author-hosted paper and supplement](https://www.princeton.edu/~mjs3/salganik_dodds_watts06_full.pdf).

**用途：** 文化选择中的社会影响、集中和不可预测性。**边界：** 特定实验不能直接替代对短视频平台算法的研究。

**Use:** Social influence, inequality and unpredictability in cultural choice. **Limit:** The experiment is not a direct study of short-video recommendation algorithms.

## R11 · 熟练判断的条件 / Conditions for skilled intuition

Kahneman, D., & Klein, G. (2009). Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree. *American Psychologist, 64*(6), 515–526. [原文 / Original text](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/kahneman-klein-2009.pdf).

**用途：** 有效学习环境与反馈的重要性。**边界：** 不把资历视为可靠判断的充分条件；M07 的动态方程是本项目示意模型。

**Use:** Learnable environments and meaningful feedback. **Limit:** Tenure is not sufficient evidence of expertise; M07 is an illustrative project model.

## R12 · 纳什均衡 / Nash equilibrium

Nash, J. F. (1950). Equilibrium Points in n-Person Games. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 36*(1), 48–49. [原始论文 / Article](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.36.1.48).

**用途：** 有限博弈中的均衡概念与存在性。**边界：** 存在性不保证唯一性、学习收敛或社会最优；M02、M03 的收益是本项目假设。

**Use:** Equilibrium and existence in finite games. **Limit:** Existence implies neither uniqueness, convergence nor optimality; M02 and M03 use assumed payoffs.

## R13 · 多中心治理 / Polycentric governance

Ostrom, E. (2009). *Beyond Markets and States: Polycentric Governance of Complex Economic Systems*. Nobel Prize Lecture. [诺奖官方原始讲座 / Official lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/).

**用途：** 跨层级、多主体制度安排。**边界：** 不把某一种治理方式当作所有 AI 问题的通用解。

**Use:** Multi-actor institutions across levels. **Limit:** No governance arrangement is presumed a universal solution for AI.

## R14 · 因果图 / Causal diagrams

Pearl, J. (1995). Causal Diagrams for Empirical Research. *Biometrika, 82*(4), 669–688. [原始论文 / Article](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/82.4.669).

**用途：** 明确因果假设与可识别性问题。**边界：** 画图本身不识别因果，箭头不是由相关自动产生。

**Use:** Explicit causal assumptions and identification. **Limit:** Drawing a graph does not itself identify causation.

## R15 · 纳什议价 / Nash bargaining

Nash, J. F. (1950). The Bargaining Problem. *Econometrica, 18*(2), 155–162. [原文 / Original text](https://www.haverford.edu/sites/default/files/Nash1950.pdf).

**用途：** 议价问题的公理化起点。**边界：** M06 使用带 β 的加权推广作为明确假设，不把加权参数当作这篇原文对实际议价权的估计。议价解与非合作博弈均衡必须区分。

**Use:** The axiomatic starting point for bargaining. **Limit:** M06 explicitly uses a weighted extension; β is not an empirical estimate supplied by this paper. Bargaining solutions differ from noncooperative equilibria.

## 引用纪律 / Citation discipline

引用理论说明“为什么这个机制值得研究”，引用实证说明“在什么条件下观察到了什么”。二者不可互相冒充。评价经验研究时，还应考察重复验证、样本范围、研究日期和利益冲突。

Theory explains why a mechanism merits study; empirical evidence states what was observed under which conditions. Neither substitutes for the other. Assessment of empirical research should also consider replication, sample scope, study date and relevant interests.
