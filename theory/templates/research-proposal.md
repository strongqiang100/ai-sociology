# 研究提案 / Research proposal

**关联编号 / Related IDs:** P… / V… / D… / M…

**陈述类型 / Claim type:** H / M / R / N

## 问题与范围 / Question and scope

说明总体、分析单位、任务、地区、期限。 / Specify population, unit, task, region and horizon.

## 定义与测量 / Definitions and measurement

分别定义输入、结果、调节、中介与混杂。 / Define inputs, outcomes, moderators, mediators and confounders separately.

## 推演与分支 / Derivation and branches

写出机制步骤、两个条件分支及最强替代解释。 / State mechanism steps, two conditional branches and the strongest rival explanation.

## 识别与反证 / Identification and challenge

说明设计、假设、测量误差及使解释失败的结果。 / Specify design, assumptions, measurement error and results that would defeat the explanation.

## 证据与边界 / Evidence and limits

提供原始出处、实际支持的论点、未解决的问题。 / Provide original sources, the claims they support and unresolved issues.

## 修订记录 / Revision history

记录日期、变化及依据。 / Record dates, changes and grounds.
