# Volume III · Derivation pool

**64 cards: 32 pairs, 20 triples and 12 quadruples.** Every card is a conditional hypothesis (H), not an empirical finding. Reference IDs identify related conceptual sources, not validation of these AI-specific claims. MODEL denotes a project accounting relation or thought experiment. Listed variables are focal inputs or moderators; outcomes appear in the branches. Background conditions are assumptions, not controls already established in data.

Review sequence: define population and horizon → establish input changes → trace the mechanism → compare branches → assess rival explanations. A third variable may mediate or moderate; a four-variable card does not assert that only four factors exist. Preregister roles for the study. Opposite results should revise the path, not trigger unlimited ad hoc conditions to protect it.

**V121 Experienced meaning · V122 Feasible choice set · V123 Future predictability · V128 Value pluralism**

**Context and mechanism：** Feasible choices and predictability shape goal pursuit, while plural values prevent a single satisfactory outcome.

**Branch A：** Acknowledged trade-offs and feasible action may sustain meaning amid uncertainty.

**Branch B and boundary：** A single success standard may narrow choices so that better forecasts do not improve meaning.

**Discriminating observation：** Combine longitudinal narratives and real choices, not income or usage substitutes; absent gains require scope revision.
